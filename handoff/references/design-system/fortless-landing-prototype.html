GOOD TO KNOW

A few things,
cleared up.

Real answers about where the platform stands right now.

Is Fortless available right now?

Not yet. This is a preview of the planned platform. Service availability, pricing and launch timing haven't been announced.

Which hosting services are first?

Game servers and Discord bot hosting are the current first-wave priorities. Other categories are planned or exploratory.

Will everything be in one dashboard?

That's the product goal: one account for purchases, billing, support and service access. Individual technical operations may still use specialized infrastructure behind the scenes.

Are the dashboard metrics real?

No. The visuals here are illustrative concepts, not connected infrastructure. They show design direction rather than an active customer environment.

Will there be VPS and domain registration?

Both are part of the intended roadmap. Launch timing depends on provider integrations, commercial arrangements and validation.

What about AI-powered features?

Assisted setup and troubleshooting are being considered, but no specific AI hosting product or capability has been committed to yet.
